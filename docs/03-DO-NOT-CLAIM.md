# Do not claim

**Binding on all UI copy, README text, the Devpost writeup and the video script.**

Six claims died during idea selection because nobody asked what a number was actually counting. Every line below is a specific error that was caught, usually after surviving several rounds of review. If a sentence you are about to ship appears here, it is wrong — not arguable.

---

## The four unit traps (all the same family — check what the number counts)

1. **Bushels are CONES, not seed.** A bushel is 8 dry gallons of opened cones. Douglas-fir yields ~0.5 lb clean seed per bushel; sugar pine ~1.4. Never write "bushels of conifer seed" — even though the AON's own conclusion section does.

2. **"Average Seeds/pounds" ≠ seedlings per pound.** The Terms of Sale header says *seeds*. The AON formula needs *seedlings per pound*. Substituting one for the other silently assumes 100% germination and 100% survival and understates seed required, plausibly by 2× or more — inside the very conversion chain that exists to demonstrate rigour.

3. **The AON's survival factor is calibrated to a "two-year seedling"; LAMRC sells one-year plugs** (minimum 9 months). Do not cross them. **Report in bushels and pounds, price off the seed list, and label any seedling figure "two-year-equivalent."**

4. **Nursery survival ≠ field survival.** The 80–90% figure is survival after outplanting and has no place in the AON chain.

## Scope and superlatives

- ❌ "California is the only state publishing such an assessment." **Never checked** — Oregon and Washington run their own seed zone systems and state nurseries. Say: *California publishes one; nobody has computed it per fire.*
- ❌ "BLM is the largest single federal buyer." Unsourced. USFS produced 1.27B seedlings in FY2023.
- ❌ "The most conservative published estimate." Say *"the published estimate least favourable to this conclusion."*
- ❌ **Anything involving BLM's 2–3M lb/yr or Audubon's "one-eighth" figure.** Confirmed: BLM's seed program is **rangeland/sagebrush-steppe** — grasses, forbs, shrubs, **no conifer seed at all**. Comparing a conifer forest's order to it compares two different markets. This survived four rounds of review before being caught.
- ❌ Competitor counts ("74 submissions", "~20 maps"). True on 17 September, stale by the deadline. Internal reasoning only.

## The 25% target

- ❌ "…to replant a quarter of its burns." ❌ "After X acres burned, California needs 55,978 bushels."
- ✅ 25% of **productive conifer forest on non-federal land, statewide**, against wildfire **plus** insect/disease mortality **plus** timber harvest.
- Do not let a pronoun ("a quarter of *that*") point back at the burned acreage.

## Seed zones

- ❌ "Seed cannot cross a zone." Zones are provisional, built on climatic similarity, with tolerances.
- ❌ "Seed *has to* match the zone and elevation." CAL FIRE's own Terms of Sale permit *"the most-suitable alternative according to the Climate-Adapted Seed Tool."* Write **"is matched to."**
- ❌ **"Seed transfer zones are species-specific."** False in both jurisdictions — California is one grid of 85 zones (Buck 1970), federal Bower is also a single generalized map. Species-specific maps exist only in OR/WA. *This was the stated Technology differentiator for four rounds and it was never true.*

## Prior art — name it before a judge finds it

- ❌ "Nobody has built a seed bill of materials." **CAL FIRE does, annually.** The honest differentiator is *annual and statewide vs per-fire and on demand*.
- ❌ "CAL FIRE does this by hand." The AON is a **GIS spatial analysis** — *"developed using a spatial approach… exported for further analytics."* Falsifiable by anyone who opens the PDF.
- ❌ Claiming climate-adapted seed matching. That's **CAST** (CAL FIRE + USFS + UC Davis). Cite it.
- Also name: **Seedlot Selection Tool** (seedlotselectiontool.org, St.Clair et al. 2022) and **Climate-Smart Restoration Tool** (USFS RMRS) — both already do assisted migration / climate-adjusted provenance. **Terraware** does forestry and nursery ops. **Regenmapper** takes a burn perimeter but answers a different question (will this regenerate unaided).

## Science wording

- ❌ "70% regenerated the prior tree mix." The source says **pre-fire density**. Density is not species mix.
- ❌ Any dispersal figure other than Gill et al. 2022 — 200 m general, 100 m non-serotinous.
- ❌ "1.4 billion seedlings produced." Fargione: **1.3 billion** produced against **+1.7 billion** needed. The 1.4 was an unrelated statistic about years of nursery inventory.
- Nursery closures are **American Forests 2021**, not Fargione.
- Seeds-per-pound: cite CAL FIRE's own Terms of Sale table, which you have.
- The 90% germination / 80–90% survival figures are **general**, not CAL FIRE's.

## Post-fire treatment (relevant only if it comes up in Q&A)

- ❌ "Post-fire spending buys nothing." **Mulch works** — 60–80% erosion reduction; log barriers 50–70%; hydromulch ~19%. Seeding is the ineffective one (78% of sites no significant reduction; 92% of the best post-2000 studies none).
- ❌ "Seeding is still the default treatment." Share of burned area seeded fell from ~21% in the 1970s to ~4% by 2000–2007 (Peppin et al. 2011). Practice already shifted.
- ❌ Implying BAER emergency stabilization money could buy reforestation seed. **Separate authorities**, confirmed — BAER vs the Reforestation Trust Fund (funded by tariffs on imported wood products, uncapped 2021).
- ❌ Any claim that hillside treatment protects downstream water quality. **Never measured** — the literature stops at the plot boundary.

## The rule that generated this list

Before any number goes on screen, ask **what it is actually counting** — the units, the population, the jurisdiction, and the year. Six claims died to that one question. Every single one looked obviously true first.

## The one gap that is a feature

CAL FIRE's own published method is **not reproducible from public data**:

> *Average seedlings produced per pound = (average seed per pound / average seed per pot) × percent survival rate in nursery × average probability of a tree in nursery*

All three right-hand factors come from *"Historical LAMRC nursery datasets"* — internal and unpublished.

**Do not paper over this. Show the three factors in amber, with explicit defaults, labelled "not published by CAL FIRE," and adjustable.** Then "What's next" is: *publish these three factors and this becomes reproducible for every state.*

You are disclosing the agency's gap, precisely, with the formula on screen — not your own.
